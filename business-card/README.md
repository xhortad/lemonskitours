# Lemonski Tours Business Card

## Files

- `front.svg` — editable layout source with linked approved logo
- `back.svg` — editable contact-side source with linked approved logo
- `front-300dpi.png` — 675 × 1125 px print preview
- `back-300dpi.png` — 675 × 1125 px print preview
- `front-print.pdf` — flattened exact 2.25 × 3.75 in front PDF
- `back-print.pdf` — flattened exact 2.25 × 3.75 in back PDF
- `preview.html` — side-by-side browser review

## Print Setup

- Finished card: 2 × 3.5 in, portrait
- Artwork: 2.25 × 3.75 in
- Bleed: 0.125 in on every side
- Color palette: Trail Purple, Trail Amber, and Trail Stone

## Before Printing

Replace these placeholders in `back.svg`:

- `PHONE NUMBER`
- `EMAIL ADDRESS`
- `WEBSITE ADDRESS`
- The sample QR graphic

Keep essential text inside the trim-safe area. Ask the printer to trim 0.125 in from every edge.
