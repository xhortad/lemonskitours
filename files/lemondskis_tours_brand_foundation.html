Storybrand Framework
The brand story
1
The character (your customer)
Vacationers, families, couples, and adventurers visiting the North Country of NH — they want to experience the real outdoors, not just a tourist checklist.
2
The problem
They have an ATV and open trails — but no local knowledge, no guide, and no idea how to turn a rental into a real memory. They worry they'll miss what makes this place special.
3
The guide (Lemonski's)
Lemonski's is the local insider — someone who actually lives and rides here. Personal, fun, and passionate about showing people the North Country they'd never find on their own.
4
The plan
Book a guided ATV tour → ride with a local who knows every trail and lookout → get photos taken and stories told along the way → leave with a full-day memory, not just miles logged.
5
The call to action
"Book your ride" — simple, direct, personal.
6
Success (what they gain)
A day they'll talk about for years — great photos, great stories, and a real connection to the North Country. Not just a ride, a full experience.
7
Failure avoided
No more "we just rode around and didn't really see anything." No missed lookouts, no wrong turns, no forgettable afternoon.
