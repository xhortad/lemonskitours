Good to know

Questions before you hit the trail?

Do I need ATV experience?+

No. First-timers are welcome. Tell us your group's experience level and we'll plan a pace that feels comfortable while keeping the day adventurous.

Do you provide the ATV or buggy?+

Lemonski Tours provides the guided tour, not the vehicle. Bring your own buggy or ATV, or rent one from a local rental business — either way, just add our tour and you're set. You drive and follow your guide.

Can you help me find a rental?+

Yes. Lemonski Tours works with local ATV rental businesses and can help point you toward a rental that fits your date and group.

What should I bring?+

Dress for changing mountain weather and wear sturdy closed-toe shoes. We'll share a complete ride-day checklist when your tour is confirmed.

What happens if the weather turns?+

We ride rain or shine. Come prepared for whatever the mountain throws at you — that's part of the adventure.
